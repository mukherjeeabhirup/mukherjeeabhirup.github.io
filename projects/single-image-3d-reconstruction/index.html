Single-Image 3D Reconstruction Pipeline

cost-effective 3D reconstruction from single 2D images for SLAM and VR applications (SRM Institute of Science and Technology, 2022)

Undergraduate research at SRM Institute of Science and Technology (May – October 2022), advised by Dr. T. Rama Rao.

  • Led development of a cost-effective 3D reconstruction pipeline from single 2D images for SLAM and VR applications, in a three-member research team.
  • Implemented and benchmarked feature-matching algorithms (SIFT, ORB) and epipolar geometry calculations using OpenCV, PyTorch, and Blender.
  • Optimized projection matrices to improve 3D reconstruction accuracy, surpassing traditional optimization baselines.
  • Developed device-agnostic image-processing techniques for rapid generation of 3D assets suitable for autonomous navigation systems.

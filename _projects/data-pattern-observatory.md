End-to-end [data observability system](https://devpost.com/software/data-pattern-observatory) built for the PNC Bank challenge at HackUTD 2024 (1,100+ participants; [North America's largest 24-hour university hackathon](https://hackutd.co/)). It visualizes data-flow patterns and flags anomalies with 85%+ accuracy as data moves across an organization.

The system runs as containerized microservices with a Streamlit front end.

**Tech:** Python, MongoDB, Redis, Elasticsearch, Streamlit, Docker.

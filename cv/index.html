Professional Summary

Broadly interested in developing interpretable and robust AI systems for security-critical applications. My current research focuses on understanding how large language models internally represent vulnerability knowledge and whether these representations can be leveraged to improve detection and repair. I am particularly interested in model interpretability and automated vulnerability analysis, as well as the security and trustworthiness of agentic AI architectures.
